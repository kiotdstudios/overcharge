OVERCHARGE LAB

Playable animation sandbox · disposable

Controls

← → or A D move — idle ↔ walk, facing
Shift run — walk ↔ run transition
↑ or W jump — jump, fall, landing
K attack — aimed cast (hold a direction to aim)
E hold absorb   Space hold charge
R reset player

These are the real game controls, read from player.js and driven through production's own input.js — jump is ↑/W, and Space is charge, not jump.

Lab-only additions, because the lab has no enemies and nothing damages you: J melee energy-strike (production plays this when an enemy is in range) and H hurt. Click the canvas first if keys do nothing.
