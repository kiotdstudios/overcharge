SYSTEM 1 - CONDUCTIVE CRATE
   Crate class in entities.js. PURE CONDUIT with zero capacity - there is no
   `charge` field on the class at all (D1). Contact is an AABB inflated by
   CRATE_CONTACT_PAD (2px, ratified); strict inequality, so exactly 2px is the
   exclusive boundary - documented and asserted rather than left implicit.
   bridgeTarget() returns ok / none / ambiguous. Delivery targets are GATES and
   SWITCHES ONLY, per Kiro's correction that ElectricalSource has no receive().
   D5 AS VETOED: ambiguous contact REFUSES, console-warns with the crate id and
   every device id, and shows AMBIGUOUS CONTACT. It never guesses.
   Push resolves inside _resolveX: destination clear -> crate and player both
   move; destination blocked -> the PLAYER is blocked. A crate cannot be shoved
   into a tile, closed gate, another crate or off the edge BY CONSTRUCTION.
   Gravity + rests on tiles, one-way platforms, movers and other crates. Solid
   to player and enemies. Crate-routed charging uses the SAME spendEnergy() ->
   device.receive() path, so there is no second copy of the transfer arithmetic
   and dormancy wake / _reactT / conservation are identical to direct charging.

SYSTEM 2 - TIMED DEVICE
   timed + duration on the existing gate. Timer starts on open; expiry sets
   open=false, charged=0 and blocking resumes. Expiry runs BEFORE the dormancy
   check in update(), so there is no one-frame flash of idle animation on the way
   down. Countdown reuses the charge-bar strip and flickers under 1s (no new
   art). _timeLeft is snapshotted so dying mid-countdown grants no free time.
   timed + isExit is REFUSED (warn + ignore) and also rejected by parity.
   Non-timed gates are provably unaffected - asserted by a 10-second regression.

KIRO'S ADDED CONSTRAINT - VERIFIED, NOT ASSUMED
   An expired timed gate is DORMANT and renders the dedicated TRUE DEAD art.
   Proven with a draw-call recorder that identifies the source image BY OBJECT
   IDENTITY: 2 simulated seconds after expiry emit exactly one variant,
   DEAD-ART, with shadowBlur 0.

PARITY GUARDS BUILT INTO THE DELIVERY (not left for gate time)
   Unique non-empty crate id, finite x/y, positive w/h when present, positive
   duration on timed gates, timed+isExit rejected, and the guard with teeth: any
   level containing crates MUST contain at least one checkpoint, because a crate
   can be wedged or pushed into a pit and checkpoint restore is the only v1
   recovery path.
   Also WIDENED the harness level scan to include the descriptive N_NAME.json
   twins, not just levelN.json. The guards were silently scanning only half the
   authored levels and my own testbed was not checked at all. That fix is most of
   the 75 -> 110 parity movement.

THREE BUGS I MADE AND CAUGHT
   1. INTERACT_RADIUS was used in player.js without being imported - would have
      thrown at runtime the first time a player walked near a crate.
   2. Test rigs assumed PLAYER_H=64; it is 30. Every push/landing assertion
      failed for RIG reasons, not code reasons - tryMoveX was correct all along.
      Diagnosed by printing real dimensions instead of adjusting the code.
   3. The draw recorder's labels were inverted: the dead art is the 9-arg
      drawImage and the OPEN sprite is the 5-arg one, so the open sprite was
      being reported as dead art. Now keyed on image identity.

DERIVED DECISION NEEDING RATIFICATION
   blockOnly gates are EXCLUDED as bridge targets. blockOnly means the player
   cannot discharge into it directly, so allowing a crate to bridge into one
   would launder energy around that authoring intent. Derived from the documented
   meaning of blockOnly, NOT from the order text. Flag if you disagree.

DESIGN CONSEQUENCE FOR LEVEL 5 AUTHORING
   With a 1-tile crate the player is usually already inside INTERACT_RADIUS
   (50px) of the gate, so the gate charges DIRECTLY and the crate route is never
   offered. My first testbed passed as playable while never exercising the crate
   at all. The testbed now uses a 64px crate and asserts energy actually flowed
   through it. Level 5 should use 2-tile crates or gates the player cannot stand
   beside, or the mechanic will not appear in play.
