Level 1 is a street run that climbs onto rooftops. It teaches absorb, carry and spend with three streetlamps and a vending machine, has an apartment with a hot tenant and an air conditioner to power (the optional Easter egg), a QuickMart, a bus stop and a few people to talk to, and ends at an exit gate that costs 8. Chief play-tested it on October 17 and called it a winner.

What changed since the October 16 roadmap.
  • The tall tower and the lift are not in Level 1. The vertical draft (tower, two ladder floors, charge-powered lift, gatehouse roof) was replaced by the level Chief built in the Builder. It stays 100 x 22.
  • The relay pylon, light cable and beacon are not in Level 1. Chief was not sold on them; Aki moved the whole set to the new Level 7. CONFIRMED by Chief, October 17.
  • The tenant sequence is built and placed. A plain, colour-matched apartment; the tenant (an adult woman with curly hair) in the window beside the ladder, so her "It's an oven in here" bubble plays as the player climbs; the AC unit at the far left of the roof (it takes the full 4, like any charged object, with no switch). Powering it plays her relief clip and the GOOD NEIGHBOR Easter egg card, whose icon pops up.
  • New life on the street: QuickMart, a bus stop with two NPCs on 8-second loops, the graffiti artist, the streetlamp woman, two smoke-break workers.
  • Charge: lamps 3 each, vending machine 2, so 11 charge is placed. The AC holds 4 and returns it when zapped. The exit costs 8.
