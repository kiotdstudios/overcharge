OVERCHARGE // Electric City Palette

Colors sampled from shipped OVERCHARGE prop art, with gameplay effect colors called out separately. Gameplay violet first, then new pipe variants and Night City props (fuse box, neon sign, security camera, streetlight, vending machine), then the core electrical gameplay props (generator, gate, switch, fence, HVAC source), then neon-rise dressing accents, live gameplay charge/HUD colors, and the fixed KIOTD brand constants. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Power Signature — Violet + Electric Blue

This is the gameplay accent palette, not a dominant-color sample of dark prop pixels. Purple belongs in the absorption and gate arcs; cyan reads as electric glow, while amber marks lamps and spent-source feedback. Values come from src_scroll/player.js, src_scroll/electricity.js, and src_scroll/energy-hud.js.
