# Neon Rise dressing pack

Eight new transparent props are in the Builder asset bank under **Filter by pack → Neon Rise**. They translate the dense neon rooftop reference into modular pieces: lightning billboard, vertical glyph sign, amber service lamp, antenna and utility cluster, cyan/magenta graffiti, service vent panel, lit conduit pipe, and chain-link railing. Search for “Neon Rise” also finds all eight.

These are visual decorations only. They have no collision, electrical, switch, or light mechanics. In particular, the chain-link railing is scenery, not the electric fence. Place them over the existing rooftop and facade tiles without changing the roof collision layout. The billboard, glyph sign, and antenna cluster suit skyline silhouettes; lamps, vents, and conduit suit the middle distance; graffiti and railing break up large blank foreground walls. Leave the gate and lower route unchanged.

Source images were generated from the user's two level screenshots as style references, then cropped and scaled into separate transparent PNGs. `assets/objects/neon-rise-dressing/source-audit.json` records the crops and output dimensions. No level files were changed for this pack.
