H hurt   R reset   B hitbox
↓ or S drop from a ledge

Real game controls, driven through production's own input.js — jump is ↑/W, Space is charge. J and H are lab-only: nothing here fights you or hurts you.

Tile edit

Turn edit on, then left-click to place a tile and right-click to erase. Drag to paint a run. Movement keys keep working, so you can build a step and climb straight out of a pit instead of resetting.

Save room keeps your layout in this browser and reloads it automatically next visit. It never touches a level file — the lab is a sandbox, so a saved room only exists here. Restore room returns to the built-in layout without deleting your save.
